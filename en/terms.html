Terms of use — Tomos

Last changed: 23 November 2026 · App version: 0.29.0

1. What this app is

Tomos is a book reader. It opens EPUB and PDF files you already have on your phone. It does not sell, lend, find or give access to books. It is one file with two versions: the free one, with ads (section 4f) and three limits, and the full one, without ads and without the limits, which one purchase through Google Play opens (section 4e).

2. The files are yours, and your responsibility

You choose which files to add. It is your responsibility to have the right to hold and read them. The app does not check, cannot check, and does not ask where a file came from.

3. Locked books

The app does not open books with copy protection (DRM), contains no means of removing such protection, and does not explain how it is done. A locked book is named as such and that is where it stops. The same goes for a PDF that asks for a password to open.

4. What it does not promise, and what always applies

We do what we can so that you do not lose books, your place in them, your bookmarks, phrases or notes, but keep your own files elsewhere too: a phone can be lost, broken or wiped, and what the app keeps lives inside it, until you uninstall the app. «Backup» puts all of it in one file, with the books too if you wish, which you put wherever you like (section 4d). From «My phrases» you can copy one phrase or note, or all of one book's; from an open book, one piece at a time, and a note's words from its box.

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes rights the law gives you that a contract cannot take away, such as those you have as a consumer and those the law provides for defective products.

The legal guarantee of conformity for digital content also applies: if the app or the full version does not do what these terms say, you have the rights the law gives you — to have it brought into conformity, a reduction in the price, or to end the contract — and the law of the country where you live may give you more.

4a. When you recommend a book to a friend

The app prepares text — the title, the author, your rating and your line — and hands it to whichever app you pick from your phone's own list. It never sends the book file, and has no way to. What you write in your line is yours, and your responsibility: we do not see it, we keep it nowhere but your phone, and we never learn who you sent it to.

4b. Reading aloud

Reading aloud is done by your phone's own voice, the speech engine the phone gives to apps (normally the one set on the phone), which belongs to its maker (usually Google or Samsung), not to us. Which voices exist, how they sound and how well they pronounce a language is up to it. Reading aloud happens only live, only for you, and is not saved as an audio file. It goes on with the screen off, and while it reads, and for ten minutes after a pause, whoever holds your phone can control it from the lock screen (see the privacy policy).

4c. When you send or copy a book's text

With «Send to an app» the app hands the text you selected, read-only, to the app you pick, usually from your phone's list (on Android 7, if only one app takes text, that app opens straight away); with «Copy», from an open book or from «My phrases», it puts it on the phone's clipboard. The other app is not ours: it has its own terms, and what it does with the text is up to it. The text belongs to whoever holds the rights to the book; you send or copy it for your own use. If you publish a quote, say which book it comes from, and do not distribute material that is not yours (section 5).

4d. The backup

The backup file is yours to keep safe: if it is lost or damaged, it cannot be restored, and if you lock it with a password and forget it, you will not be able to open it, and nor can we open it for you. A restore replaces what the app holds on the phone with what the backup holds, and says so first. A backup «With the books» holds your books' files: it is for you, for your own phones. If you give it to someone else, you give them the books too, and their rights are not yours to give (section 5).

4e. The full version and buying it

The free version has all of reading, the library with its covers, the backup, the move to a new phone and copying, and it shows ads. Its three limits: you keep up to 10 bookmarks, phrases and notes together; it does not read aloud, nor has the «Screen lock» that goes with reading aloud; and it has no shelves. The full version has no ads and none of these limits. What you made stays as it is, in either version, however many marks you have; the shelves you set up in the full version stay as you left them and show again with it.

It is opened by one purchase, inside the app, through Google Play, at the price Google Play shows at the time of the purchase, VAT included. It does not move to another account, and it does not go into a backup. On the phone where you have it, it opens without a connection too; to get it back in a new install, Google Play is needed.

The full version opens as soon as the payment is complete.
