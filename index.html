Tomos: EPUB & PDF reader

EPUB and PDF reader, two-page spreads; reads aloud in the full version.

Get it on Google Play

  • Opens EPUB and PDF files you already have on your phone.
  • Books locked against copying (DRM) and PDFs with a password do not open.
  • In the full version: reading aloud, in EPUB only, with the phone's voices that work without the internet (the languages depend on the phone), without bullets, squares and other symbols or the captions the book marks; a «Sleep timer» of 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes or to the end of the page or the chapter; speed 0.5× to 2× and pitch, with − and +.
  • Bookmarks in EPUB and PDF, and in EPUB words you keep as a «Phrase» or a «Note» in your own words, by book in «My phrases»; in the free version up to 10 in all, and the ones you have stay.
  • In EPUB books you select words with two handles, and the selection goes on to another page with the bar's «Previous page» and «Next page».
  • The words you select you copy, or hand with «Send to an app» to an app you choose.
  • PDFs have no word selection, phrases, notes or search.
  • Your own colours for the page and the letters, in EPUB and PDF (in PDFs the photos change too); a pair that would be hard to read is not taken.
  • You choose the colour of bookmarks, phrases and notes, and see them above the page slider; you make categories of phrases in «Marks», with a name, a colour and a marker or an underline, and choose them with «Category» on the selection bar.
  • In «My phrases», the covers, and in each its phrases by category and page; you copy one, or all with «Copy all from this book».
  • In a book, drag up the handle on the bottom bar: all its marks, in page order, and you go to whichever you tap.
  • Three book fonts, or your phone's own, and «Bold», in EPUB books.
  • You choose the bar's buttons and their order, and add «Night» if you wish; «Settings» and «Bookmarks» always stay.
  • «The screen stays on» for 5, 10 or 30 minutes, if you wish, in EPUB and PDF.
  • The book's contents, EPUB or PDF, when it has them, take you to the chapter you choose.
  • Pictures in EPUB books, and the one you hold full screen, with zoom; not SVG drawings, very large pictures, very small ones within a line or large moving ones; other moving pictures stay still, tables show as text, and books made only of pictures do not open.
  • Search in EPUB books, with or without capitals and accents (Greek and most European Latin letters).
  • You put the books in your own order: hold a cover and drag it.
  • In the full version, shelves too, with «Show the shelves»: books upright or flat, spine out, with the title and, in EPUBs that give them, the author and the publisher, as far as they fit, and a thickness that follows their length, or cover out; up to 100 shelves with «Add a shelf», in an «Oak», «Walnut» or «White» look; you drag the books from shelf to shelf and they stay where you leave them, even with a gap beside them; zoom with two fingers.
  • A book starts again whenever you like, and its marks stay.
  • «Backup», in «About, privacy and licences», makes one file with your library, marks and settings, with or without the books, with a password if you wish, which you put wherever you like; «Restore from a backup» brings them back, on this phone or another.
  • A small card at the end of the book, in EPUB and PDF books.
  • Your books and what the app keeps about your reading do not leave your phone by themselves: there is no sync and no copy in Google's cloud.
  • The free version shows ads at two moments at most: when the app opens, not the first few times, and when you go back from a book to the library, not every time; never while you read.
  • The full version, with one in-app purchase: no ads, reading aloud, bookmarks, phrases and notes without the free version's limit of 10, and the shelves.
  • No account.
  • The app speaks Greek, English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish and Polish: as the phone does, or as you choose.
